## What's new in 0.2.0

### Languages
- **Mickey now speaks French**, following the macOS language. English remains the fallback. More languages can be added without touching the code ([`docs/LOCALIZATION.md`](https://github.com/Arrows78/Mickey/blob/main/docs/LOCALIZATION.md)).

### Accessibility
- **Reduce Motion** is honored: click effects, the shake locator, the ring pulse, the focus glide and the on-screen displays fade in place instead of moving.
- Sliders, pickers, color wells and the annotation toolbar are **named for VoiceOver**, and the selected tool, color and page are announced.
- New **Contrast Outline** option for the ring: a thin black or white edge keeps it visible on any background.

### Annotations
- **Redo** with ⇧⌘Z and a toolbar button.
- **Copy as Image** with ⌘C or the toolbar button: a transparent image of your drawing, cropped to what you drew.

### Recorder
- Choose the **quality** (Full HD, QHD or 4K) and the **frame rate** (30 or 60 fps). Defaults are unchanged: 4K at 60 fps.

### Settings
- **Live Preview** of the spotlight and the ring on a light and a dark background, at the top of Cursor Effects.
- **Export and import** your settings as a JSON file, to back them up or move to another Mac. Your custom click sound stays on the Mac it was chosen on.
- **Automatic Mode** can use a different preset for each watched app.
- Setting labels are wider so longer translations no longer wrap.

### Good to know
- The sandbox entitlement for user-selected files is now read-write, so the export can be saved.
- Settings saved by earlier versions load unchanged; new options start at their defaults.
